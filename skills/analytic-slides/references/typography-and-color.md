# Typography and color

Type scale (pt): title 28, subtitle 15, entry heads 14, body 12 (auto-shrinks to 10, never lower), captions 12-13, ticks/footer 9. Theme `paper` = Times New Roman (matches the manuscript figures); `sans` = Arial.

Palette (colorblind-checked, shared with `multipanel-figures`): blue `#3a7ca5`, green `#009E73`, vermillion `#D55E00`, amber `#E1A730`, purple `#7B4FA3`, slate `#3d4d5c`. Text `#1d2a35`, muted `#5b6672`, title navy `#16324F`.

Never reuse one color for two concepts on a slide, and keep an object's color across slides. Do not shrink text to fit: remove redundancy, simplify labels, switch layout, or split the slide.
